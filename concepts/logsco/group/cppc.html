Consultation & expertise

Plan the centre before the machines.

A collection, process and packing centre (CPPC) brings fruit from many growers into one place to be cleaned, graded and packed. We help estates, agencies and packers plan one: volumes, layout, line and support.

Planning, step by step

Seven questions to answer first.

Machinery selection is step four, not step one. This is the order we work through with you.

  1. Step 1

    Volumes & season

    Daily and peak intake, harvest season, crop and fruit sizes.

  2. Step 2

    Grades & markets

    Who buys the fruit, which grades and packs they expect.

  3. Step 3

    Site & building

    Floor length and width, access for trucks, expansion room. Reference: the Kulim line needs a factory at least 34 m long.

  4. Step 4

    Flow & layout

    Intake to dispatch in one direction, with no crossing of clean and dirty areas.

  5. Step 5

    Utilities

    Power, water and drainage for the wet section, confirmed on site.

  6. Step 6

    People & training

    Operators and maintenance staff, trained during two months of commissioning.

  7. Step 7

    Support

    24/7 technical support and 3–5 day spare parts after handover.

FAQ

CPPC questions

What does CPPC stand for?
Collection, process and packing centre: a facility where fruit from many growers is received, cleaned, graded and packed before going to market.
Do you design the building?
We plan the line layout and the flow of fruit through the building with you. Civil and building works are scoped separately and to be confirmed per project.
How accurate is the sizing tool?
It is indicative. It divides your daily intake by operating hours and compares the result with our 7 MT/hr Kulim reference line. Real sizing depends on fruit size, grades, packing format and site.
Can a CPPC start small and grow?
Planning for expansion from day one is one of the first things we discuss: building length, utilities and where extra sections or lanes could go.
