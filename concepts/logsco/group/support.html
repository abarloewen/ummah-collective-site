When something goes wrong

Four steps back to running.

  1. 1

    Report

    Call, WhatsApp or send a service request with the machine, the issue and photos.

  2. 2

    Diagnose

    A technician diagnoses remotely where possible, or arranges a site visit.

  3. 3

    Dispatch

    Spare parts are sourced and dispatched. Our standard is 3–5 days.

  4. 4

    Fix and test

    The part is fitted and the line is tested before we sign off.
