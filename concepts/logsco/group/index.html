Project highlight · Kulim (Malaysia) Berhad

Watch a pineapple go from tank to box.

A Reemoon wet-and-dry pineapple line: seven sections in one pass, built for export-grade fruit under the LPNM × Kulim export programme. Tap a station to see what it does.

Simulation7 MT/hr reference capacity · 34 m min. factory lengthSwipe for all 7 stations →
  1. Station 1 of 7 · Wet section

    Washing

    Fruit is tipped into a washing water tank and lifted out on a roller elevator, removing field soil and debris before any treatment.

    Washing water tank · roller elevator
  2. Station 2 of 7 · Wet section

    3-layer coating

    Three coating stages treat the fruit surface. Waxing is one of the line’s core capabilities and protects presentation on the way to export markets.

    Three-layer coating process
  3. Station 3 of 7 · Dry section

    Drying

    An air-drying machine removes surface moisture after coating, so fruit is dry before it reaches weighing and inspection.

    Air-drying machine · roller conveyor
  4. Station 4 of 7 · Dry section

    Weighing

    Every fruit is weighed in-line. The weight reading drives weight-based sorting further down the line.

    In-line weighing system
  5. Station 5 of 7 · Dry section

    Scanning

    An internal quality detector looks inside each fruit for water core and translucency, defects you can’t see from the outside.

    Internal quality detector
  6. Station 6 of 7 · Dry section

    Grading & separation

    A single-lane sizer sends each fruit to the right outlet by grade and weight, so every outlet carries a consistent product.

    Single-lane sizer
  7. Station 7 of 7 · Dry section

    Packaging

    Disc packers, a weighing platform and roller and belt conveyors bring graded fruit to the packing team and out to dispatch.

    Disc packers · weighing platform · conveyors
