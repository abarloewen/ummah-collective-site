FAQ

Questions buyers ask

What can we monitor with IoT?
Soil and climate sensors report field conditions, and drones give an aerial view. Dashboards bring readings together remotely. The exact sensors are selected for each estate.
Is the dashboard on this page real data?
No. It is an illustration with sample values, to show the kind of view an estate dashboard gives.
How does AI-vision grading work?
Cameras on the line inspect each fruit and sort it into grades such as Grade A, Grade B, processing or reject. Configuration is agreed per project.
Can IoT connect to our grading line?
That is the aim of a connected estate: field data, line data and cold-chain data in one view. Integration is scoped during consultation.
Where can we see it?
At our Pontian estate, which uses smart irrigation and IoT field monitoring and doubles as a live showroom.
