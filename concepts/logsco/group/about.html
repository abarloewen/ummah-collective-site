Home/About

Your partner in agricultural machinery, and more.

Logsco Agri Technology Sdn. Bhd. was established in 2025 as a progressive offshoot of Logsco Sdn. Bhd., a Malaysian-owned logistics company. Seeing significant opportunities in the national agricultural sector, and aiming to diversify the group’s revenue, Logsco launched this dedicated company to distribute agricultural equipment and machinery, backed by international manufacturing partners and local technical support.

Mission

To deliver high-quality agricultural equipment, provide exceptional support services and implement effective solutions that significantly enhance the productivity and efficiency of Malaysia’s agricultural sector.

Vision

To become Malaysia’s premier leader in innovative and sustainable agricultural machinery solutions.
