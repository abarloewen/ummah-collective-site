Your partner in agricultural machinery, and more.

Logsco Agri Technology Sdn. Bhd. was established in 2025 as a progressive offshoot of Logsco Sdn. Bhd., a Malaysian-owned logistics company. Seeing significant opportunities in the national agricultural sector, and aiming to diversify the group’s revenue, Logsco launched this dedicated company.
