FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Which HUAYO AGRO machines do you supply in Malaysia?
We select from HUAYO AGRO’s field and production range to suit each operation. The current Malaysian range is confirmed on request: tell us the crop and the task.
Who services the machines?
Our own technicians. Installation, commissioning, maintenance and repairs are handled locally, with 24/7 technical support.
How fast can we get spare parts?
Our standard is a 3–5 day spare parts turnaround, so machines don’t sit idle for weeks.
Can we see machinery working before we buy?
Our Pontian estate in Johor is a live showroom where we demonstrate machinery and IoT in real field conditions. Book a demo visit.
Do you train operators?
Yes. Operation and maintenance training is part of every delivery.
