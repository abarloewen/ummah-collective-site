About Pina Boosta

Pina Boosta is a 150 ml pineapple-based functional energy booster from Johor, Malaysia, made with pineapple elements from Malaysian-grown fruit, including fruit from its own estate in Pontian. Positioned around natural energy, mental focus and plant power, Pina Boosta launches in Malaysia first with a limited, numbered Batch 001. Pina Boosta is a brand of Logsco Beverages, a division of Logsco Agri Technology Sdn. Bhd.
