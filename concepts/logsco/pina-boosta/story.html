The journey

Five steps. One fruit.

Grown

Pineapple grows on our estate in Pontian, Johor, with smart irrigation and field monitoring.

Harvested

Picked ripe, and graded so only the right fruit goes on to the next step.

Made

Pineapple elements from Malaysian-grown fruit go into every batch.

Bottled

150 ml, sealed, chilled and ready when you are.

Shared

Malaysia first, then out into the world.

Why pineapple

Malaysia grows world-class pineapple. We think it deserves a world-class brand.

Malaysian pineapple already travels the world as fresh fruit. Pina Boosta takes it further: a drink with a Malaysian name on the label, made for local shelves first and global ones next.
