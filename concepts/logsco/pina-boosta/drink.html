The drink · what’s inside

Pineapple, bottled.

Pina Boosta is a 150 ml functional energy booster made with pineapple elements from Malaysian-grown fruit. Here’s everything we can tell you so far, and what’s still to be confirmed.

The three benefits

What it’s for.

01

Natural energy

A 150 ml functional booster built around pineapple, for the moments when the day needs a second wind: the 3 pm dip, the drive home, the last set.

Energy profile and active ingredients to be confirmed with the final recipe.

02

Mental focus

Made for the long meeting, the exam week and the last hour of the shift. Small enough to keep in your bag, cold enough to wake up your desk.

Formulation details to be confirmed with the final recipe.

03

Plant powered

Pineapple elements from Malaysian-grown fruit, including fruit from our own estate in Pontian, Johor. The pineapple isn’t a flavour on the label, it’s where we start.

Full ingredient list to be published before launch.
