About us

Your partner in agricultural machinery.

A focused agri-technology division with the backbone of an established Malaysian logistics group.

Who we are

Logsco Agri Technology Sdn. Bhd. was established in 2025 as a progressive offshoot of Logsco Sdn. Bhd., a Malaysian-owned logistics company.

Seeing significant opportunities in the national agricultural sector, and aiming to diversify the group’s revenue, Logsco launched this dedicated division. We specialise in the distribution of agricultural equipment and machinery, backed by international manufacturing partners and local technical support.
