03

THE GAP

Today every stage looks like a page view

01

No funnel events

GTM fires on page load and consent only. A booked assessment, a submitted application and a paid fee are not recorded as conversions.

02

The payment leaves the site

The admission fee is paid on Stripe's checkout or the PayPal link. Browser tags lose the family at that step.

03

Source isn't saved with the lead

No UTM, click id or referrer is kept. “Heard from” offers Friends, Family or Social Media.
